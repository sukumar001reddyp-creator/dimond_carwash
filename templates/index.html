Step-by-Step Technique

How We Do Waterless Wash

Our precise multi-step procedure to keep your car immaculate and protected.

Step 1 & 2

Shade & Towels

Make sure the vehicle is parked in a shaded area and keep two clean microfiber towels ready.

Step 3 & 4

Shake & Spray

Shake the Auto Wash bottle and spray solution onto one folded microfiber towel until moist.

Step 5 & 6

Surface Application

Spray directly onto a designated surface area and softly wipe with the moist towel.

Step 7 & 8

Immediate Buffing

Immediately buff off any light haze with the second dry microfiber towel until polished.
